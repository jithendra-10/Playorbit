FunStack Privacy Policy

Last updated: [27/10/2026]

This policy explains how the FunStack Android app ("FunStack", "the app"), published by [PlayOrbit Studio], handles information. If you have questions, contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL].

Information we store on your device

FunStack saves your game progress, scores, settings (such as language, theme, sound and ad-personalisation choice), token balance and Ad-Free Pass status on your device. We, the developer, do not run servers and do not receive this information. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include this data in your Google account backup so it can be restored on a new device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

FunStack shows ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and app information, ad interactions, and crash or performance diagnostics. This information is collected by Google, not by us. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent using Google's consent tool before personalised ads are shown. You can turn off personalised ads in the app's settings, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The app receives a confirmation from Google Play so it can unlock what you bought.

Children

FunStack is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

Because your game data is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. For data collected by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy controls at myaccount.google.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the "Last updated" date above.
